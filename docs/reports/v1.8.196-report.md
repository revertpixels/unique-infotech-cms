## 1. What Was Built

1. **Whole-Month Planned Break Management (Unique Kids)**
   - Admins (`superadmin`) can put an active Kids student on a planned whole-month break (`startMonth` to `plannedEndMonth` + required `reason`), inspect unpaid dues + late fines before confirming, and later record their return (`Resume / Rejoin` with an exact `returnDate`).
   - Built a dedicated Break Management console (`#/school-break`) with KPI cards (**On Break**, **Break Ended - Not Rejoined**, **Rejoined**, **Total Records**), deep links from Dashboard cards (`?filter=active`, `?filter=overdue`), search/status filtering, pagination, and audit trails (**Started by**, **Resumed by**).
   - Added break status badges (`On Break (<Period>)`, `Rejoin overdue`) and break history cards inside Student Profiles (`#/students/<id>`) and Kids Admissions rows.

2. **Course Upgrade / Package Change & Selectable "First Monthly Billing Month"**
   - Added `First monthly billing month` (`createBillingStartSelector`) to both **Kids Admissions** (`#/school-students`) and **Upgrade Course / Change Package** (`openKidsUpgradePackageModal`).
   - Restricted the dropdown strictly to **two allowed choices**: **Next month** (`+1`) and **Month after next** (`+2`).
   - Automatically resets the default selection whenever the admission payment mode changes:
     - **Full payment** (`full`) or **Difference payment** (`difference`) -> defaults to **Month after next** (`+2`, leaving a 1-month gap).
     - **No payment** (`none`) -> defaults to **Next month** (`+1`).
     - Admins can still manually choose the other allowed option.
   - Enabled `No payment` for returning/rejoining admissions on `#/school-students` and formatted exemption reasons on admission receipts (`school-receipt.js`).

3. **Read-Only Access for `school_head`**
   - Granted `school_head` read-only access to Break Management (`#/school-break`) and Unique Kids Student Profiles (`#/students/<id>` for `ctx === 'school'`).
   - Action buttons (`+ Start Break`, `Resume`, `Upgrade`, `Collect`, edit controls) remain hidden for `school_head` and enforced by `firestore.rules`.

4. **Speed & Caching Parity Across All 8 Unique Kids Pages (`>= 300ms` Delayed Skeletons)**
   - Built shared session caching, `updatedAt` delta sync, server count aggregates, N+1 query removal, and `>= 300ms` delayed skeletons across all 8 Unique Kids views so switching between Kids pages paints cached views immediately (`3ms–170ms` observed in Chrome + Firebase Emulator tests) without skeleton flicker.

## 3. Data Model Fields & Collections

- **`students` (`ctx: 'school'`)**:
  - Break fields: `status` (`'active' | 'break'`), `activeBreakId`, `breakFrom` (`YYYY-MM`), `breakTo` (`YYYY-MM`), `breakReason`, `breakStartedBy`, `breakApprovedAt`, `breakReturnDate` (`YYYY-MM-DD`), `breakReturnMonth` (`YYYY-MM`), `breakResumedBy`, `breakResumedAt`, `breakHistory[]` (`{ breakId, from, to, actualReturnDate, actualReturnMonth, effectiveEndMonth, endedEarly, reason, status, by, byName, resumedBy, resumedByName, at, resumedAt, billsCancelled }`).
  - Billing-start & upgrade fields: `firstMonthlyMonth` (`YYYY-MM`), `defaultFirstMonthlyMonth` (`YYYY-MM`), `firstMonthlyChangedFromDefault` (boolean), `activeEnrolmentId`, `upgradeDifferencePaid`, `packageHistory[]`.
- **`breaks`**:
  - `{ id, ctx: 'school', studentId, studentCode, studentName, startMonth, endMonth, plannedEndMonth, effectiveEndMonth, actualReturnDate, actualReturnMonth, endedEarly, reason, status ('active' | 'completed'), billsWaived, startedBy, startedByName, startedByDesignation, startedAtISO, startedAt, resumedBy, resumedByName, resumedByDesignation, resumedAtISO, resumedAt, createdAt, updatedAt }`
- **`bills`**:
  - Deterministic open-bill IDs `${studentId}_${YYYY-MM}`. When paused for a break, unpaid future/current break-month bills transition to `status: 'break-waived'`, `breakId`, `cancelReason`, `stoppedAt`, `stoppedBy`. Upon rejoin (`resumeKidsBreak`), bills from `rejoinMonth` onward revert to `status: 'unpaid'` (full monthly fee).
- **`enrolments`**:
  - Tracks active vs upgraded level enrolments (`status: 'active' | 'upgraded'`, `billingTo` set to `addMonthsYM(billingStartMonth, -1)` on upgrade).
- **`payments`**:
  - Records upgrade payments (`type: 'UPGRADE'`) and monthly bill allocations (`billId`, `month`, `amount`, `feeCovered`, `lateFeeCovered`, `receivedDate`, `receivedBy`, `receivedByName`, `recordedByDesignation`).
- **`students/{id}/payment/payment`**:
  - Stores admission receipt summary (`studentId`, `receiptNo`, `admissionFee`, `configuredAdmissionFee`, `monthlyFee`, `actualAmountReceived`, `paymentMode`, `noPaymentReason`, `noPaymentOther`, `firstMonthlyMonth`, `defaultFirstMonthlyMonth`, `firstMonthlyChangedFromDefault`). Modern student docs also duplicate the inline fee & receipt fields, allowing `loadKidsPaymentHistory` to avoid N+1 subcollection reads.

## 4. Billing, Break & Rejoin Rules

1. **No Partial / Day-Based Fees:** Every billed month charges 100% of `monthlyFee`.
2. **Whole-Month Breaks Always Start Next Month or Later:**
   - Current month is always billed in full (`minStartMonth = addMonthsYM(currentMonth, 1)`). Mid-month requests auto-set `startMonth` to next month and inform the admin that the current month is charged in full.
   - Any pre-existing unpaid bill inside `[startMonth, plannedEndMonth]` is updated to `status: 'break-waived'` (`₹0`). Partially paid bills are preserved.
   - While `status === 'break'`, `syncOpenKidsBills` skips generating bills, and `evaluateKidsPendingBills` excludes students on break from Pending & Overdue.
3. **Rejoining / Returning from Break (`resumeKidsBreak`):**
   - Admin selects the exact `returnDate`; `rejoinMonth = returnDate.slice(0, 7)` is billed **in full** regardless of the day of month.
   - **Early Return:** If `rejoinMonth <= plannedEndMonth`, break completes at `rejoinMonth - 1`, break-waived bills from `rejoinMonth` onward are restored to `unpaid`, and open months up to current month are billed. Months after return inside the original plan become normal billable months.
   - **On-Time / Late Return (`Ended - Not Rejoined`):** Once `currentMonth > plannedEndMonth` while still on break, no monthly bills are generated for the gap months (`isBreakOverdueReturn === true`). When the student eventually rejoins, normal full-month billing resumes starting from `rejoinMonth`.
4. **First Monthly Billing Month (Admissions & Upgrades):**
   - Strictly two selectable options: `nextMonth` (`+1`) or `afterNextMonth` (`+2`).
   - Mode defaults: `full` / `difference` -> `afterNextMonth` (`+2`); `none` -> `nextMonth` (`+1`). Changing payment mode resets the dropdown to that mode's default.

## 5. Speed Changes Per Page (All 8 Unique Kids Pages)

All 8 pages share `window.APP_CACHE.financialReports` (`ensureReferenceData`, `ensureKidsDataset`, `syncKidsDatasetDelta`, `kidsPending`, `kidsHistory`) with `CACHE_STALE_MS = 30000` (30s) and `scheduleDelayedSkeleton(..., 300)`:

1. **Dashboard (`dashboard.page.js`, `#/dashboard` in `school` mode):** Paints immediately from cached `kidsDataset` + `kidsHistory`; on cold load runs `loadKidsDashboardServerAggregates` (3 `getCountFromServer` queries with 1-time silent fallback) in parallel and delays loading placeholder 300ms.
2. **Students (`school-admissions.page.js`, `#/school-students`):** Paints immediately from `getCachedReferenceData` + `getCachedKidsDataset`, background-syncs via `syncKidsDatasetDelta` only when stale, listens to `uic:data-mutated`/`uic:meta-mutated`, and delays `#adm-loading` by 300ms.
3. **Break Management (`school-break.page.js`, `#/school-break`):** Reuses `getCachedKidsDataset()` (`0ms` warm load), delta-syncs when stale, cleans up listeners on `unmount()`, and delays `#skb-loading` by 300ms.
4. **Collect Fees (`school-fees.page.js`, `#/school-fees` & `school-fee-payment.page.js`):** Reuses shared `meta` + `kidsDataset`, delays `#sf-loading` by 300ms, dispatches `uic:data-mutated` on payment, and runs background delta sync if stale or `billsSyncedAsOf !== today`.
5. **Pending & Overdue (`pending.page.js`, `#/school-pending` + `report.service.js`):** Added instant `getCachedReferenceData` + `getCachedKidsPending` paint, refactored `loadKidsPendingReport` onto `ensureKidsDataset`/`syncKidsDatasetDelta`, and set Kids skeleton delay to 300ms (Youth remains 250ms).
6. **Payment History (`payments.page.js`, `#/school-payments` + `report.service.js`):** Added instant `getCachedReferenceData` + `getCachedKidsHistory` paint, refactored `loadKidsPaymentHistory` onto `ensureKidsDataset`/`syncKidsDatasetDelta`, removed N+1 `payment/payment` subcollection reads for modern inline docs, and set Kids skeleton delay to 300ms.
7. **Levels & Fees (`courses.page.js`, `#/courses` in `school` mode):** Reuses `getCachedReferenceData` / `ensureReferenceData`, completely skips querying Youth `sessions`, and delays loading state text by 300ms.
8. **Student Profile (`student-profile.page.js`, `#/students/<id>` for Kids):** Immediately hides `#sp-loading` and arms a 300ms timer (`scheduleDelayedSkeleton`), reuses `ensureReferenceData` (no Youth `sessions` query), paints hero/metrics/tabs immediately from `kidsDataset` + `kidsHistory` when cached, and reads inline admission receipts in `loadPayments` instead of re-fetching subcollections.

## 6. Known Limitations & Pending Deploy Items

- **Pending Firebase Deploy:** Updated `firestore.indexes.json` (5 new Kids composite indexes; `32` total composite indexes + `1` override) and `v1.8.196` web assets are committed locally / pushed to `main`, **NOT yet deployed** to live `unique-infotech-cms` (`hosting` or `firestore:indexes`).
- **Auto-Bill Generation Is Client-Triggered:** Because no Cloud Functions are used on the Spark/client architecture, `syncOpenKidsBills` runs client-side when a `superadmin` loads Dashboard, Students, Collect Fees, Pending & Overdue, or a Student Profile.
- **Root Kids Collections:** `bills`, `breaks`, `payments`, and `waivers` remain root collections queried once per dataset load/delta because legacy live documents may omit `ctx` or `updatedAt`. `syncKidsDatasetDelta` automatically falls back to a full `ensureKidsDataset` reload if >25 student docs lack `updatedAt` or if collection document counts indicate deletions/missing timestamps.
