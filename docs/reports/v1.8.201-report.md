## 3. What Was NOT Copied from Youth's `#/pay-fees` to Kids `#/school-fees` (and Why)

1. **Session Badge, Session Selector Scoping & "No Youth session found" Gate:** Youth Centre runs fixed academic sessions; Unique Kids uses continuous month-to-month billing without sessions, so Kids shows **"Monthly (1st open · due 10th)"** and **`Head Office (HO)`**.
2. **Board Examination Fee Toggle & Exam Management Sub-View (`#pf-exam-view`, Exam Fee Locking Table):** Unique Kids programmes do not have Board Examination Fees or exam-fee locking.
3. **Strict Sequential-Only Installment Lock:** Youth `#/pay-fees` forces unpaid installments to be collected strictly one at a time in order (`—` on later installments). Kids `#/school-fees` allows collecting any individual open monthly bill (**Collect**) OR paying all unpaid months together in one transaction (**Collect all due**).
4. **Total Course Fee / `% Collected` Progress Metric:** Because Kids fees continue month to month while enrolled rather than summing to a fixed course total, the 4 Kids metric cards show **Monthly Fee Rate**, **Paid Months**, **Unpaid Fee Due**, and **Late Fine Today** instead of total package progress.
