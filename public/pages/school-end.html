End Course

End the student's current enrolment. Student ID, history, bills and payments are preserved. No future bills will be generated after the ending month.

Select a student from All Students to continue. Open All Students and choose a student, then use End Course from their row.
–
Existing unpaid bills remain payable. Ending does not waive them.
This action cannot be undone. The student can be re-admitted later with the same Student ID.
